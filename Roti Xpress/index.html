About us

Roti Xpress is a Brampton-based wholesale bakery specializing in fresh, authentic rotis. Backed by generations of traditional baking experience, we supply high-quality rotis daily to tiffin and catering services, food trucks, restaurants, events, and retail customers across the Greater Toronto Area and Ontario.

Using quality ingredients and consistent daily production, we deliver soft, fresh rotis made for both bulk orders and everyday family meals. Our commitment to freshness, consistency, and dependable service has made us a trusted partner for businesses, event organizers, and local families.

Questions about our rotis

Ordering rotis for a tiffin service, a restaurant or an event? Here are the answers to what people ask us most.

Who do you supply rotis to?

We supply fresh rotis to a wide range of businesses and customers, including tiffin services, caterers, restaurants, food trucks, grocery stores, banquet halls, religious events and retail customers. We make thousands of rotis every day and take orders of any size, from a small family order to large daily wholesale orders. Call us to set up a one-time or regular order.

Do you make tiffin meals?

We focus on what we do best: fresh rotis. Tiffin services serve our rotis alongside their own sabzi, dal and curries. If you run a tiffin service or are looking for rotis for your home tiffin, give us a call.

Can I order rotis for a party or event?

Yes. We make rotis for parties, family functions, religious events and other occasions, in any quantity. Call or email us with the number of rotis and the date and time you need them.

What kinds of rotis do you make?

Punjabi rotis (thick and soft), Gujarati rotis (thin and light), whole wheat rotis (100% whole grain) and puris. Our Punjabi and whole wheat rotis are made with Sher Atta, canola oil and ghee.

How do I place an order and get pricing?

Call us at 647-576-0822 or email xpressroti@gmail.com. We are open every day from 7 AM to 8 PM. We will confirm your price and timing, and wholesale pricing is available for bulk and regular orders.

What areas do you serve?

We serve customers across the Greater Toronto Area and other cities in Ontario, including Brampton, Mississauga, Vaughan, Toronto, North York, Milton, Oakville and Orangeville. Not sure if we cover your area? Give us a call.

Ready to order?

Call or email us with what you need and when you need it. We'll confirm your price and timing.
